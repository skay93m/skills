# Recall

## Steps

### Read the Governing Documents

Read SOUL.md, ACADEMIC_STANDARDS.md and PARKING_LOT.md from the working directory by bare name; stop before proceeding if any is missing. Shared rules in those documents govern this skill.

### Frame the Retrieval Probe

Select or confirm one legal doctrine, statutory provision, or ethical concept for the retrieval probe, such as Bolam and Bolitho, Montgomery materiality, Mental Health Act 1983 s 63, or Mental Capacity Act 2005 capacity. Ask the student to recall it from memory, including its rule or test, primary authorities, and boundary conditions or analytical tensions. Ask whether they are ready before starting.

### Enforce the Five-Minute Sprint

Ask the student to start a 5-minute timer and write continuously as an unprompted recall from memory without notes, reading materials, or external databases. This 5-minute micro-scaffolding limits fatigue and executive paralysis. Rough notes, bullets, and fragments are acceptable. Await the submitted text.

### Conduct the Flag-Only Audit

Compare the response with verified primary authorities and required criteria. Medical Law 2 includes Bolam, Bolitho, Montgomery, Chester, Re MB and Re B; end-of-life law includes Airedale NHS Trust v Bland, Nicklinson, Conway, the Suicide Act 1961 and treatment withdrawal; forensic services includes Mental Health Act 1983 ss 2, 3, 37, 41 and 63, Mental Capacity Act 2005 ss 1 to 4, and ECHR Articles 2, 3, 5 and 8. Report only diagnostic flags: present, gap, and conflation, one focused challenge at a time, and never substitute prose. Ask how the student would address a gap or conflation.

### Capture Concessions and Tangents

State each concession as `Concession: ...` in the session reply for session notes or blueprint, and record concessions for session notes, recording what was done and what comes next. Capture tangents in PARKING_LOT.md under its rules, then return to the sprint. If a timestamp, write, or readback fails, reply, "The parking-lot item was not saved: <the unsaved item>." Lock one immediate next action.
