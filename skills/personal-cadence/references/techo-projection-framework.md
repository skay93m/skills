# Jibun Techo 3-Phase Projection & Tracking Framework

> [!NOTE]
> This reference is terminal (1-level deep from entrypoint). Do not link to other markdown reference files.

This framework defines the structured temporal discipline of the Jibun Techo 24-hour vertical layout, adapting Japanese planner methodology for ADHD executive function support and daily momentum architecture.

---

## 1. Principles of the 24-Hour Vertical Canvas

The Jibun Techo format structures the day as an unyielding continuous canvas (00:00 to 24:00), making the finite nature of time visually tangible.

- **Dual-Stream Layout**:
  - **Left / Projected Stream**: Morning intentional allocation of time blocks (how time ought to be invested).
  - **Right / Actual Stream**: Real-time or evening capture of actual time spent (how time was truly invested).
- **Visual Categorization**:
  - Deep Work (Cognitive Focus): Solid color or bounded block.
  - Shallow Work & Operational Admin: Striped or light boundary.
  - Life Maintenance & Health: Distinct physiological marker.
  - Rest, Restoration & Buffer: Unallocated white space.

---

## 2. The Three-Phase Projection Ritual

### Phase 1: Morning Intention (07:00 - 08:30)
1. **Sleep Audit**: Log wake-up time, recorded sleep duration, and subjective physical readiness (scale of 1 to 5).
2. **Primary Momentum Vector**: Identify the single indispensable milestone for the day. If only one outcome is accomplished, what creates legitimate momentum?
3. **Block Projection**: Map 2 to 3 focused work blocks onto the vertical timeline, ensuring realistic 15-minute transition buffers flank every major cognitive block.

### Phase 2: Daytime Focus Blocks & Check-Ins (09:00 - 18:00)
1. **Transition Anchors**: Mark the start and conclusion of each block with physical or visual cues.
2. **Ebb & Flow Logging**: When energy flags, note the interruption or friction point beside the timeline rather than abandoning the plan.
3. **Midday Re-Anchoring**: At the midday boundary, adjust afternoon projections if morning execution deviated from initial estimates.

### Phase 3: Evening Debrief & Reconciliation (18:30 - 21:00)
1. **Plan vs Reality Reconciliation**: Fill in the right-hand actual column against morning projections. Calculate total deep-focus hours achieved.
2. **Micro-Win Capture**: Record at least 3 completed concrete actions or learnings, counteracting the ADHD negativity bias.
3. **Tomorrow Onramp Setup**: Leave the desk or workspace in a "ready-to-ignite" state with tomorrow's starting file open and activation steps staged.

---

## 3. Executive Function Scaffolding Rules

- **Buffer Compounding**: Never schedule consecutive deep work blocks without minimum 15-minute white-space buffers. Cognitive switching costs deplete executive stamina.
- **Pencil Over Ink**: Treat projections as provisional navigation vectors, not moral obligations. When an unexpected derailment occurs, calmly re-anchor from the current hour rather than discarding the day.
- **Visual Reality Checks**: If planned activities exceed the available vertical hours, prune secondary tasks immediately.
