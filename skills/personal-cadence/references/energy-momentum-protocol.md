# ADHD Energy & Momentum Scaffolding Protocol

> [!NOTE]
> This reference is terminal (1-level deep from entrypoint). Do not link to other markdown reference files.

This protocol provides cognitive scaffolding, circadian energy-to-task pairing, and tactical interventions to defeat executive dysfunction, preserve momentum, and navigate energy transitions.

---

## 1. Circadian Energy Mapping

Cognitive capacity is non-linear. The protocol maps work to physiological energy phases rather than arbitrary clock intervals.

- **Peak Mental Energy (PME) Window (Typically Morning / Early Afternoon)**:
  - *Cognitive State*: High executive bandwidth, working memory stability, sharp pattern recognition.
  - *Task Pairing*: Deep architectural design, formal proofs, complex refactoring, postgraduate legal reasoning.
  - *Constraint*: Strictly protect from transactional communications, emails, or unscheduled meetings.

- **Post-Prandial Dip Window (Typically Early Afternoon)**:
  - *Cognitive State*: Lower vigilance, slowed processing speed, susceptibility to distractibility.
  - *Task Pairing*: Low-stakes administrative tasks, mechanical dependency updates, code formatting, physical walks, restoration.
  - *Constraint*: Do not attempt high-activation or ambiguous design problems.

- **Evening Consolidation Window (Late Afternoon / Early Evening)**:
  - *Cognitive State*: Moderate, relaxed focus; reflective synthesis.
  - *Task Pairing*: Reviewing PR feedback, logging daily accomplishments, organizing tomorrow's staging files, reading documentation.

## 2. Executive Dysfunction & Low-Activation Onramps

Executive paralysis occurs when the activation energy required to start a task exceeds available working memory and emotional activation.

### The Two-Minute Micro-Step Technique
When facing task initiation paralysis:
1. **Reduce Scope to the Absurd Minimum**:
   - Instead of "Write Chapter 2", the target becomes: "Open the editor and write one complete sentence."
   - Instead of "Implement the Deep Module", the target becomes: "Create the manifest YAML file."
2. **Remove Environmental Friction**:
   - Pre-open required files, terminals, and documentation before commencing.
   - Close non-relevant browser tabs and silence notifications.
3. **Ignition Over Completion**:
   - Once the physical motion of typing or editing begins, the neurological threshold of task-initiation is crossed, allowing dopamine pathways to engage naturally.

## 3. Hyperfocus & Cognitive Fatigue Detection

Hyperfocus is a powerful asset that carries severe downstream risks if unmonitored: cognitive depletion, biological neglect, and subsequent multi-day burnout.

### Detection Indicators
- Ignoring physiological cues (hunger, thirst, muscle tension, eye strain).
- Tunnel vision on minor cosmetic details while ignoring core milestone boundaries.
- Reluctance to stop even when diminishing returns and error rates spike.

### Intervention Protocol
- **Hard Interval Enforcers**: Enforce 50-minute focus blocks paired with mandatory 10-minute away-from-screen breaks.
- **Cognitive Battery Check-In**: Rate internal energy from 1 (exhausted) to 5 (energized). If battery falls below 2, immediately initiate a restorative break rather than pushing through.
- **Graceful Context Exit**: When a work session ends, capture immediate thoughts in a "scratch pad" note to permit cognitive disengagement without fear of losing train of thought.
