# Quiz

## Steps

### Read the Governing Documents

Read SOUL.md, ACADEMIC_STANDARDS.md and PARKING_LOT.md from the working directory by bare name; stop before proceeding if any is missing. Shared rules in those documents govern this skill.

### Select the Curriculum Topic

Identify and confirm one module and topic: Medical Law 2 (clinical negligence, autonomy, or risk disclosure), Law at the End of Life (treatment withdrawal, assisted dying, or best interests), or Specialist Forensic Services (admission criteria, s 63 treatment, or human rights). Focus on one substantive issue. Use this primary-authority and framework inventory as the grounding checklist, inherited from the predecessor retrieval skill and verified against the governing documents before auditing: Medical Law 2 uses Bolam, Bolitho, Montgomery, Chester, Re MB and Re B; Law at the End of Life uses Airedale NHS Trust v Bland, R (Nicklinson) v Ministry of Justice, R (Conway) v Secretary of State for Justice, Suicide Act 1961 section 2, the doctrine of double effect and withdrawal of life-sustaining treatment; Specialist Forensic Services uses Mental Health Act 1983 sections 2, 3, 37, 41 and 63, Mental Capacity Act 2005 sections 1 to 4, ECHR Articles 2, 3, 5 and 8, and Rabone v Pennine Care NHS Foundation Trust.

### Pose the Socratic Challenge

Ask one sharp Socratic challenge exposing a legal tension or doctrinal conflict. Invite a rapid three-to-five-minute diagnostic free-write that states the student's resolution and primary authorities.

### Audit the Diagnostic Free-Write

Audit using the flag-only boundary. Check whether the student identified the tension, omitted statutory thresholds or appellate rulings, grounded assertions, distinguished ratio decidendi from obiter dicta, and avoided assuming consensus where courts remain divided. Ask one follow-up question about the weakest point.

### Log Concessions and Exit

State each concession as `Concession: ...` in the session reply for the student to carry into `/blueprint` or session notes, and record concessions for the blueprint, recording what was done and what comes next. Capture tangents in PARKING_LOT.md under its rules; if a timestamp, write, or readback fails, reply, "The parking-lot item was not saved: <the unsaved item>." End cleanly and return the student to the primary deliverable.
